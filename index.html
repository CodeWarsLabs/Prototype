Globi Adventure — Reading Prototype

The Welcome screen shows the GLOBI logo, a short introduction and the Join the Globi Adventure button. Then sign in with your name, age and school, tap Acknowledgment and OK, then make your Globatar: choose a character, skin and eyes, and tap Confirm. Every character keeps its own original outfit and original shoes, exactly as drawn.

On the world screen, all seven continents are labeled: Africa is highlighted as the first destination and the others — Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Australia and Antarctica — are locked. Drag the globe sideways to spin it through the continent names. Your own Globatar stands in the top corner. The Avatar, Books, Games and Snap Words buttons are the buyer's clean coloured buttons with their words fully visible and nothing on top of them.

The World Map button — or tapping Africa on the globe — opens the flat Map of the World: a framed map with all seven continents labeled (North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia and Oceania, Australia and Antarctica), Africa highlighted with a star, the others locked with number pins, Quest, Videos, Books, Songs and Games tabs along the top, and your own Globatar in a profile card in the top-right corner. Tap Africa on the map to explore it.

Click on Africa, then click on Kenya: a short animated introduction plays with facts about Mount Kenya, elephants and lions, Lake Victoria and Nairobi. Then your safari friend walks into the scene and the phonics lesson plays: hear each CVC word — cat, bus, mud, fan, pig and top — sounded out, then tap the word you are asked for; right and wrong answers get instant feedback. Tap Play to start Level 1.

In Level 1 you can also drop down into a lower valley — the screen follows you down — and collect letter stars that say their letter sounds. In the Skill Check, the stars and gems you earned are shown at the top, and the microphone button records your voice saying the word and plays it back to you. The level-completed screen shows your total stars and gems, with Next Level, Menu and Play Again buttons.

On the Avatar screen, the Boy and Girl buttons next to your Globatar choose your gender. The yellow profile button at the top right of the world screen opens your Globi Passport: it shows your Globatar photo, your name, your status as a Globi Jr., your gender — tap the gender pill to switch between Boy and Girl, and the Avatar screen changes too — passport number GLOBI-001 and Globian nationality, and your eye colours. Tap Back to return to the world screen.

In the game, use the left and right arrows (or the Arrow keys) to walk and the JUMP button (or Space) to jump. Collect all 15 stars and all 5 superpower gems. At the bamboo ladder, tap and hold the ladder itself to climb up to the top platform — hold the lower part of the ladder to climb back down — or swipe up or down on the screen while at the ladder (on a keyboard, hold Arrow Up / W to climb up and Arrow Down / S to climb down). When every star and gem is collected, the Skill Check opens: tap the missing vowel letter to complete each word.
